README - Project Overview

During my project I decided I want to display how through natural beauty occurence and symbolism that nothing lasts foever, in this instance the beauty of petals dying and decaying. 

Overtime I had to rotate every petal individually, which took it's time creating a full, presentable and identifiable look of a flower. I did enjoy this as I knew it would be very satisfactory when I had completed this, and it really was.

The project I have created, I set during Spring due to the bright rather than dull green background, as logically petals fall off during Autumn so I have created a sped up process for the life and death, of a system.

In many religious beliefs they believe that growth is only created to be taken down and reborn again to become a bigger, fuller and more beautiful plant with even more flowers every year of bloom. Specifically reincarnation in the aspects of becoming a different creature, mamal or species.

I have made the flower slanted to emphasis the affect even further of the plant and petals being seen to be pushed by the wind, one of natures weather elements, to be realistic and not forced.

Therefore, I believe my project represents all of these aspects of religion and cultural beliefs through to physical elements.

From a technical point of view I have used two shape commands and multiple petals to show the individual rather than bulk presentation, of the rotation that was necessary for the project. Thus, reflecting my time and effort put into the different aspects I wanted to execute.
